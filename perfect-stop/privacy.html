Privacy Policy

Perfect Stop: Tap Timing · SMART HESAP
Last updated: October 28, 2026

Summary

Perfect Stop does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The game saves a few settings locally on your device only: your best level, number of games played, sound and haptics preferences, and selected language. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Game Center

On iOS, if you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the level you reach in each game and your achievements are sent to Game Center so they can appear on the leaderboard under your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store any Game Center data.

Network access

The game works fully offline and does not send any data to our servers or to third parties.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds optional features), the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
